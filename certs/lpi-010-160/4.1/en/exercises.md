Guided exercises for topic 4.1 written to `certs/lpi-010-160/4.1/en/exercises.md`, matching the English format used by the other topics (e.g. 3.3) and mirroring the scope of the existing Spanish version at `certs/lpi-010-160/4.1/es/exercises.md`.

The material contains six guided exercises with numbered hands-on steps and comprehension questions after each block:

1. **Identify your distribution and its lifecycle** — `/etc/os-release`, `uname -r`, EOL and LTS concepts.
2. **Rolling release vs. fixed releases** — Debian Stable vs. Arch Linux upgrade models.
3. **Distribution families and package managers** — `apt`/`dnf`/`zypper`/`pacman`, parent/derivative distributions, RHEL rebuilds.
4. **Linux beyond the desktop** — Android's kernel, Raspberry Pi OS, the TOP500 supercomputers.
5. **Comparing Linux with Windows and macOS** — drive letters vs. single tree, Unix heritage, shells, licensing.
6. **Choosing a distribution for a concrete scenario** — four case studies (school, bank, hobbyist, sysadmin) plus TCO and support-model questions.

All answers are collapsed in a `<details>` section at the end, and the LPI Learning Materials URL is cited as the reference source.
